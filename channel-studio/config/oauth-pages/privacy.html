Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

ICYFOX US Tool ("the tool") is a private application used only by the owner of the ICYFOX US YouTube channel.

Data the tool accesses

With the owner's permission through Google OAuth, the tool uses YouTube API Services to access the owner's YouTube channel information, video details, captions and analytics reports, and to upload and update the owner's videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to report channel statistics to the owner and to manage the owner's own videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. Data is deleted when the owner removes it or revokes access.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

Third-party terms

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
