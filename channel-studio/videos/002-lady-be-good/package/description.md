In 1958, oil prospectors flying over the Libyan Desert spotted an American B-24 bomber lying on the open sand, hundreds of miles from where anyone thought it could be. Lady Be Good had vanished on her first mission in April 1943 with nine men aboard. This is how she got there, what her crew did next, and what the searchers found, told from U.S. Air Force, National Archives and Air & Space Forces Magazine records.
